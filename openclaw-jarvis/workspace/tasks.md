# Tasks

General to-dos. Commitments (with a time and escalation) live in
`state/commitments.json`, not here. Format: `- [ ] task · due DD/MM · tag`.
Jarvis marks `[~]` started and `[x]` done, and never deletes a line without
asking.

## Homework

## Other
